Manager Style Map

Each dot is one manager's spell at one club, placed by how the team played: 14 opponent-adjusted measures of passing, pressing and build-up. Arrows follow the 33 mid-season manager changes. Most arrows are short, and most new managers inherit the style as well as the squad.

Style shift relative to normal in-season drift. The gray band is the 95% range at placebo points, where the manager did not change. Orange dots fall outside it.

Method. Metrics are adjusted per league for opponent and home advantage (metric ~ team + opponent + home). "PC1" and "PC2" are the first two principal components of the 14 spell averages. A change's style shift is the root-mean-square across metrics of (after − before) ÷ the metric's spread at placebo points, using up to 10 matches either side. Caretakers with ≤ 3 matches are skipped.

Data: StatsBomb open data. Code and full report: the coach-style project in the De-Zerbi-research repository.
